# LumenFilm Camera (`com.lumenfilm.camera`)

**LumenFilm** is a hardware-adaptive analog film & computational Camera2 application for Android (Android 8.0+), engineered to scale automatically across entry, mid-range, and flagship Android hardware.

- **Website & Policy Pages**: https://aman451645.github.io/LumenFilm/
- **Latest Android APK**: [Releases](https://github.com/Aman451645/LumenFilm/releases/latest)

## Features
- **32 Original Film Looks**: 18 real-time 3D LUT film stocks (`helios`, `solstice`, `velvet`, `nordic`, `silver`, `prism`, `kinetix`, `tungsten`, `verdant`, `coastal`, `horizon`, `terra`, `nocturne`, `metro`, `xenon`, `amber`, `archival`, `prologue`) + 14 curated recipes across 8 ISO qualities and 10 tonal curves.
- **Universal Camera2 Multi-Lens Discovery**: Automatically detects physical/logical Ultra-Wide, Main, Telephoto, and Front sensors with real 35mm-equivalent focal labels.
- **Hardware-Adaptive Pipeline**: Profiles CPU, RAM, OpenGL ES float FBOs, and Camera2 level (`ENTRY` / `BALANCED` / `PERFORMANCE` / `ULTRA`) for smooth live preview on any Android model and full RAW/DNG + 3-frame Smart HDR + optical halation shaders on flagships.
- **On-Device AI Composition Coach**: Offline Google ML Kit subject/scene detection, 31 structural framing guides, 3-Tap Liner, and post-shot composition review.
