About LumenFilm

LumenFilm is an independent mobile photography application developed and published by Aman Sahu in India. We build original, offline-capable computational camera software designed to bring physical film color science and compositional guidance to a wide range of Android devices.

How delivery works: LumenFilm is distributed digitally via Android APK releases. After installing the app, users select a plan inside the app and complete payment via Razorpay (UPI apps including PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, plus debit/credit cards and net banking). Access activates digitally within seconds upon payment confirmation.
