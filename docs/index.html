About LumenFilm

LumenFilm is an independent mobile photography application developed and published by Aman Sahu in India. We build original, offline-capable computational camera software designed to bring physical film color science and compositional guidance to a wide range of Android devices.

How delivery works: LumenFilm is a digital camera software service. Inside the LumenFilm app, users start a 7-day free trial or select a plan and complete payment via Razorpay UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM). Upon payment confirmation, access activates digitally within seconds and the LUMEN-... license key is delivered both inside the app and to the registered email address.
