Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LumenFilm (“we”, “our”, “us”), operated by Aman Sahu, handles information when you use our Android camera application (com.lumenfilm.camera) and website.

1. Photos, Camera & Media Storage

  • All photo processing, film LUT color grading, HDR blending, and on-device ML Kit composition analysis happen locally on your Android device.
  • We do not upload, view, store, or train AI models on photos or camera frames you capture.
  • Camera, Microphone (optional shutter/audio features), and Storage/Media permissions are used solely to operate the camera viewfinder and save your photos to your device gallery.

2. Subscription, Email & Device Licensing Data

When you purchase or activate a paid subscription plan (Lumen Lite, Plus, or Pro), we collect and process only the minimum data needed to issue and verify your license:

  • Account Email Address: Used to associate your license key, billing receipts, and support requests.
  • One-Way Hardware Device Digest (Fingerprint): A cryptographic SHA-256 hash generated locally on-device to enforce device seat limits (1 device for Lite, up to 2 devices for Plus and Pro) and prevent unauthorized APK sharing.
  • APK Signing Digest: Verified locally and during license checks to protect against tampered builds.

3. Payments

Payments are processed securely by third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay for Indian UPI/cards/netbanking or Stripe for international cards). We do not collect or store your card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or bank login credentials on our servers. Payment processors handle your transaction details under their own PCI-DSS compliant privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Subscription license records are retained while your plan is active and for statutory billing/accounting compliance. You may request account or license data deletion at any time by emailing us via the details on our Contact Us page.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions or data deletion requests, please reach out through our Contact Us page.
