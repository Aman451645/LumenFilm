Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

LumenFilm provides instant digital access to Android camera subscription features upon payment confirmation. Because all plans unlock digital software immediately, please review our cancellation and refund terms below.

1. Subscription Cancellations

  • You may cancel your monthly or yearly subscription at any time before the next renewal date by contacting support with your registered account email or managing any recurring mandate in your UPI/payment app.
  • After cancellation, you will continue to retain access until the end of your current paid billing period, after which access locks automatically with no further charges.

2. Eligible Refunds

Refund requests are honored within 7 days of purchase in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment or accidental double charge for the same subscription period.
  • A critical technical compatibility issue on your Android device that prevents the camera from launching and cannot be resolved by support.
  • Failed payment debit where the subscription license was not activated.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Partial-month unused time once digital software features have been activated and used, except where required by applicable consumer protection law.
  • Licenses terminated due to unauthorized app tampering or exceeding device-sharing seat limits.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are initiated within 48 hours of verification and credited back to the original payment method (UPI, bank account, or card) within 5–7 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email your registered account email address, transaction ID / Razorpay payment reference, and reason for request via our Contact Us page.
